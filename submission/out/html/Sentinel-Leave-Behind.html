What Sentinel is. One registry, one map and one viewer for Gujarat's government cameras, with plate reading where a camera's view allows it and vehicle and person counts everywhere else. It is Model 2 (unified viewing and selective analytics) on the mandatory Model 1 foundation (registry and GIS): it connects to cameras, recorders and VMS re-streams directly, installs nothing on a department's side and replaces nothing. Video stays with the departments; only metadata (plate reads, counts, alerts) crosses the network, and every search and operator action is audited. No facial recognition, by design.

Architecture

Sized for 80,000 cameras by a capacity model (bench/capacity.py, Plan for Scale): 248 edge GPU nodes, about 2.7 Mbps of metadata statewide against 320 Gbps to carry every stream centrally, ₹100.7 crore over five years (₹210 per camera per month). Tier shares, streams per node and unit prices are assumptions, labelled as such; the pilot measures them. The core was run at statewide size on one laptop: 706 sightings a second matched against 100,000 watchlist entries, a floor, not a sizing (core_scale.md).

Said plainly

  • VAHAN and eGujCop are mock adapters over fixture files behind one adapter contract; SARTHI, AFIS and NAFIS are declared, awaiting access.
  • Plates are read on 2 of the 30 sandbox views. The other 28 are overview or red-light views where plates are too small; they are counted, and the Health page says what would make them readable (zoom, shutter, infrared, re-aim). The grade describes the camera; it does not predict whether a given read is right.
  • People are counted, never identified. An intrusion alert holds the zone, the count and the dwell, never a box, image or track.
  • Since the submission (26 Sep → 10 Oct): the counts-only worker and the two-laptop set-up were built and measured; the "check the crop" confidence marking passed its pre-registered gate on 8 Oct and is on; the OCR fine-tune was non-inferior on real plates and not adopted; the YOLOX detectors were not trained (the pilot's plan). Every accuracy, capture and latency figure above still describes the build that runs.

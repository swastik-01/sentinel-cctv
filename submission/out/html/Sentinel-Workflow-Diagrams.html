Statewide architecture (target design)

Department estates are untouched; district edge nodes relay and analyse video; only metadata reaches the state core, and a plate crop when an alert or search asks for it. Store-and-forward is built and tested in the PoC; the event bus, database standby and crop object store are design.

Integration workflow: onboarding a camera by stream URL

The camera sees one session; credentials never leave the server; a department user can onboard only their own department's cameras. Measured: 1.2–2.0 s end to end on an idle stack (17 Sep).

Analytics workflow: from RTSP frame to watchlist alert

Hardware decode where available; plate detection and OCR, and vehicle and person detection, on the same frames; a per-character vote across each track's reads; a repeat filter; matching and alerting. Laptop measurements, 24 Sep.

Sentinel · Gujarat Police Innovation Challenge 2026 · figure 4 of 8

Alert workflow: from sighting to closed alert

Open alerts in severity order; near misses labelled; a plate added to the watchlist is checked against the last 24 hours. Every operator action is audited.

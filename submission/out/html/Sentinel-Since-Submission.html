Since the submission: 26 September to 9 October 2026

An addendum to the jury pack. The pack submitted on 27 September describes commit 9349f8f; the finale runs the tree committed before the 10 October freeze (2572c82 of 8 October, plus the 7–8 October reliability and security fixes and this document pass). This page says what changed between the two, what was measured, what was tried and not adopted, and what runs on the day. Every number names the script-written file it comes from; nothing here is estimated. Written 8 October 2026; updated 9 October.

What was tried and not adopted

  • The OCR fine-tune ft1: non-inferior on real plates, no gain (the table above), so the shipped reader (cct-xs-v2-global-model) runs at the finale. bench/results/ocr_ft1.md.
  • 25 reader changes without training (test-time augmentation, contrast, nine other models and ensembles, two-line halves, upscaling): already in the pack; none met its pre-set gain rule on real plates. bench/results/ocr_fix_trials.md.
  • G0, G2, G2b, G4, G5, G6 (bench/pipeline_gates.md): not built, so not adopted.
  • YOLOX detectors (bench/detector_gates.md): not trained. Both detectors remain Ultralytics YOLO, AGPL-3.0, and every figure in the pack is theirs.

The index of all of this is bench/results/pipeline_gates_results.md.

What the finale runs

Two GTX 1650-class laptops. Laptop A: database, API, relay, the control room on the projector, and the plate worker on a fixed group of plate cameras at full rate (4 cameras at ~4.5 fps with object detection, bench/results/latency.md; or 8 at 2.9 fps with the plate path alone, bench/results/decode.md). Laptop B: the counts-only worker on every other camera from keyframes, the judges' hands-on console (operator login), and the hot spare (a database dump and the crops every 10 minutes to a USB stick; scripts/db_restore.ps1 brings the control room up on B). No rotation. The plate pipeline is the 26 Sep build plus G3's marking; the plate detector runs at 640 px; the reader is unchanged.

What is unchanged, and what is still unmeasured

Unchanged since the submission: real plates 90 of 120 exact (75.0%), 107 within one character, one blind reviewer, two readable sandbox views of 30 (bench/results/sandbox_review.md); at confidence 0.9 and above 69 of 81 exact; stock footage with synthetic plates 96.8% exact and 91.2% capture on AMD-TRAF-002 (anpr_eval_current.md); alert latency median 1.06 s, p95 1.94 s (latency.md); 30 of 30 sandbox cameras onboarded, 1.0–2.2 s each (sandbox.md). The two sandbox runs are the 25 Sep evening run and the 26 Sep morning run of the same looping footage, not day and night (sandbox_daynight.md). The capture grade describes a camera and does not predict a right read (capture_quality.md).

Still unmeasured, and said so: capture on real plates; count accuracy (per-frame COCO detections, no auto-rickshaw class, night misses); a plate worker and the counts worker on one machine; streams per server node (the Plan for Scale's 250 per tier B node stays an assumption; one laptop took 40).
